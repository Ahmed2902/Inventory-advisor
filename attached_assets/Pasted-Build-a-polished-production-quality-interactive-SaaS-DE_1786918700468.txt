Build a polished, production-quality **interactive SaaS DEMO** for an ecommerce intelligence product.

This is NOT the real production SaaS yet.

The goal is to create a convincing clickable prototype that can be shown to real ecommerce business owners to validate whether they would use and pay for the product.

Do not integrate with real Shopify, Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok, billing, authentication providers, or external APIs yet.

Use realistic mock data throughout.

# PRODUCT CONCEPT

The product connects ecommerce inventory data with paid advertising performance.

It helps ecommerce owners answer:

**“Given my inventory, sales velocity, incoming stock, and advertising performance, which products should I scale, hold, reduce, or stop advertising?”**

The target user is primarily a small/mid-sized ecommerce founder or owner who:

* owns physical inventory
* runs a Shopify store
* runs Meta Ads
* manages advertising personally or with a very small team
* does not have a dedicated inventory planner/data analyst
* wants very clear recommendations rather than complicated analytics

The product should feel like:

**an intelligent decision assistant between Shopify inventory and Meta Ads.**

Do NOT make it feel like:

* a generic ERP
* an inventory spreadsheet
* an ad manager clone
* an AI chatbot
* an enterprise analytics system

The value proposition is:

> **Know which products you can safely scale before your inventory runs out.**

Alternative supporting copy:

> Turn your Shopify inventory and Meta performance into simple growth decisions.

# IMPORTANT PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

The application should NOT merely tell the merchant:

“Stock is low.”

Shopify already provides inventory visibility.

Instead, the product should combine:

* inventory
* recent sales velocity
* historical sales velocity
* advertising spend
* advertising performance
* incoming inventory
* estimated stock coverage

and produce an actionable recommendation:

* SCALE
* INCREASE
* HOLD
* REDUCE
* PAUSE

For this demo, all calculations can use deterministic mock logic and pre-generated realistic data.

Do NOT pretend the demo contains a sophisticated real AI forecasting model.

Where appropriate, describe results as:

* estimated
* projected
* likely
* confidence range

instead of falsely precise predictions.

# IMPORTANT UX DETAILS

The demo must answer these questions within approximately 10 seconds of opening:

1. What products are in danger?
2. What products can I push harder?
3. Why?
4. What happens if I increase advertising?

The owner should NOT need to understand:

* machine learning
* statistical forecasting
* attribution theory
* inventory science

Use plain business language.

Bad:

**Multivariate demand elasticity forecast**

Good:

**At this spend level, you're likely to run out before restock.**

Bad:

**SKU-level time-series inference**

Good:

**Sales accelerated over the last two weeks.**
